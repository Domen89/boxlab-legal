# Privacy Policy — BoxLab

_Last updated: 12 May 2026_

## Summary

BoxLab is designed with full respect for your privacy. **We do not collect,
store, or transmit any personal data.** All calculations and user data
(drivers, projects, measurements) stay on your device. The app works
completely offline.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Specifically:

- No registration or account data
- No usage analytics or tracking
- No crash reports that include personal data
- No sharing with third parties
- No advertising

## Permissions used

- **Microphone** — used exclusively for the "SPL Meter" and "Measurement"
  features. The captured audio is processed in real time, is **never**
  saved or transmitted, and you can revoke the permission at any time
  from your device settings.

## In-app purchases

When you buy BoxLab Pro, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple
(App Store) or Google (Play Store). BoxLab receives only a binary
"purchased / not purchased" confirmation and stores it locally on your
device. We do not receive payment information, addresses, or identifiers.

## Local storage

Your drivers, projects, systems, and settings are stored in your
device's app-private "shared preferences" area. They are deleted when
you uninstall the app. They are not synced to any cloud or automatic
backup.

## GDPR rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing to request,
modify, or delete on our side. To erase the local data it is enough to
uninstall the app.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page
and at the public URL. Significant changes will be announced in an app
update.

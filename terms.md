## What BoxLab is

BoxLab is an offline engineering calculator for loudspeaker enclosures and
passive crossovers. It predicts behaviour from published acoustic theory —
Thiele/Small, Small's 1974 passive radiator model, Webster's horn equation,
standard filter alignments — and plots the result.

It runs entirely on your device and makes no network requests. There is no
account, no login, and nothing you create is shared with other users.

## What BoxLab does not promise

Every number BoxLab shows is **a prediction from a model**, not a measurement
of your cabinet.

- Real enclosures depart from theory. Panel thickness, bracing, damping,
  leakage, baffle diffraction, driver sample variation and room placement all
  move the result.
- The maths is only as good as the parameters you type. A wrong Vas or Qts
  produces a wrong box, and the app has no way to know.
- The SPL meter uses the phone microphone, which is not an IEC 61672 certified
  instrument. Readings are indicative, roughly ±3–5 dB without calibration.
  **Do not use BoxLab for occupational noise assessment, for legal evidence, or
  anywhere a certified sound level meter is required.**

Check the output before you cut wood, spend money, or promise a result to
someone else. Verifying a design before building it is your responsibility.

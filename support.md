# Support — BoxLab

BoxLab is a loudspeaker design calculator: enclosures (sealed, bass reflex,
bandpass, passive radiator, horn), passive crossovers, 3D cabinet dimensions,
SPL measurement and PDF export.

## Contact

**Email: ungaro444@gmail.com**

Write in English or Italian. Replies usually within 2–3 working days.

When you report a problem, it helps a lot if you include:

- the device and OS version (e.g. iPhone 15, iOS 18.2);
- the app version (Settings → About);
- which module you were in (Sealed box, Bass reflex, Crossover, …);
- the driver parameters you entered, or a screenshot of the screen.

## Frequently asked

**I bought Pro and it is gone / I changed phone.**
Open the Pro page from the banner on the home screen and tap **Restore
purchases**. The purchase is one-time and tied to your Apple ID (or Google
account), so it restores on every device signed in with the same account. If it
still does not restore, email us with the date of purchase.

**The app asks for the microphone.**
Only when you open the SPL Meter or the Room Measurement page. The audio is
processed on the device to compute sound pressure level and the 1/3-octave
spectrum. Nothing is recorded, stored or transmitted. You can revoke the
permission at any time in the system settings.

**Do my projects leave the phone?**
No. BoxLab works entirely offline and stores projects on the device. There is
no account, no analytics and no advertising. See the
[privacy policy](https://domen89.github.io/boxlab-legal/).

**The numbers do not match another program.**
Tell us which program and which value. Most differences come from a different
alignment family, a different leakage Q (Ql), or from modelling the real driver
impedance instead of an ideal resistor. We are happy to go through the formula
with you.

**I would like a feature / I found a wrong result.**
Email us. Reports with the driver's Thiele/Small parameters attached get fixed
fastest.

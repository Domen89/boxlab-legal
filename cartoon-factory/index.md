# Cartoon Factory

Cartoon Factory is a personal, non-commercial tool that produces short animated
videos about everyday curiosities — how the human body works, why people behave
the way they do, how ordinary objects around us work — and publishes them to its
owner's own YouTube channel, **Thengm Studio**.

It is operated by a single person for a single channel. It is not a service, it
has no users other than its owner, and nothing is offered to the public beyond
the videos themselves, which anyone can watch on YouTube.

## What it does

* Writes a short script and renders an animated vertical video.
* Uploads that video to its owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube
  Data API, and sets its title, description, tags and thumbnail.

That is the whole of it. It reads nothing from other people's accounts and
publishes nowhere else.
